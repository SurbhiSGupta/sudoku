Help

Get started

  1. Fill the board so every row, every column and every 3×3 box has the numbers 1 to 9, each just once.
  2. Tap a square, then tap a number below the board.
  3. Not sure yet? Turn on Notes and pencil in the numbers that could fit.
  4. Each wrong number uses up one of Frisky's dogs. You can change the limit, or hide mistakes, in Settings.
  5. Every puzzle has exactly one answer, so you can always solve it with logic. No guessing needed.

Handy buttons

  • Time: tap it to pause. The board hides so the clock is fair.
  • Done: tap it for a hint. The first tap points at a square; tap again to see its number.
  • Undo and Redo: step back and forward through your moves.
  • Number First (Settings): pick a number, then tap or drag across every square it goes in.
  • Focus Mode (Settings): hides everything but the board and the number pad.
  • Tap her (the stick figure) for a little story with Frisky.

Today's puzzle

A new puzzle every day, the same one for everyone. Solve it on the day to keep your streak going. Missed one? Play it from the calendar any time.
