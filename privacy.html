🧩 Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

This policy covers the Sudoku app for Android and the Sudoku game at sudoku.bodaijewels.com, made by Surbhi Gupta. The short version: Sudoku does not collect, share or sell any personal information.

What the app collects

Nothing. There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The app does not send your information to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game saves your current puzzle, notes, timer and settings on your own device. This never leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (on the website, when you clear your browser's site data).

Permissions

  • Vibration: for light taps when you place a number and when you win.
  • Internet: included by the app framework. The game itself works fully offline and does not use it to send any data.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses when the page is loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the app ever adds features that use data, such as ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
