🧩 Bodai Sudoku Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 21, 2026

This policy covers the Bodai Sudoku app for Android and the Bodai Sudoku game at sudoku.bodaijewels.com, made by Surbhi Gupta. The short version: Bodai Sudoku does not collect, share or sell any personal information. It sends anonymous play stats, such as which level was played and how long it took, so the game can get better. You can switch them off in Settings at any time.

What the app collects

No personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in and no ads, and nothing you type ever leaves your device.

Anonymous play stats

To learn which levels and features people enjoy and where puzzles get too hard, the game sends anonymous events, for example "a Fetch puzzle was finished in 6 minutes with 1 hint", "Dark theme was chosen" or "a Wild card was turned down". They include:

  • a random ID made on your device (not linked to you, your account or your phone number)
  • how you play: levels, times, mistakes, hints, and which features and settings you use
  • the type of device and screen size, and in the app, the phone's maker and model and the app version
  • an approximate country and city, worked out from the connection when the event arrives

The stats are stored by PostHog in the European Union, and used only to improve Bodai games. They are never sold or used for ads. Turn them off any time in Settings → Share anonymous play stats; nothing more is sent after that. If your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal, they start off.

What stays on your device

So you can pick up where you left off, the game saves your current puzzle, notes, timer and settings on your own device. It also keeps your solve history (date, level, time and mistakes) there to show your stats. None of this ever leaves your device. It is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its data (on the website, when you clear your browser's site data).

Daily reminder

If you turn on the daily reminder, your phone shows a notification at the time you pick. It is scheduled on your phone; nothing is sent to us. Android asks for your permission first, and you can turn it off in Settings at any time.

Backup

The app keeps a copy of your stats and settings in its own storage on your phone. If you have Android backup turned on, Android may include it in your phone's backup to your Google account, like other app data, so it comes back when you set up a new phone. We never see this backup.

Bug reports

If you tap Report a bug in Settings, the app opens your own email app with a draft to us. The draft has a screenshot of the game and a few details to help fix the problem: your phone model, Android version, text size, app version and the puzzle you were playing. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send that email, and you can edit or remove anything first.

Support Bodai pack

The optional Support Bodai pack is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. Google Play handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the pack, so it can unlock it (and bring it back on a new phone). If you use a code instead, the code is checked on your device and nothing is sent anywhere. Your chosen outfit, colors and pack status are saved on your device like your other settings.

Permissions

  • Vibration: for light taps when you place a number and when you win.
  • Google Play billing: so you can buy the optional Support Bodai pack. Nothing is charged unless you choose to buy it.
  • Internet: to send the anonymous play stats described above (unless you turn them off). The game itself works fully offline.

The website

The web version is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log technical details such as IP addresses when the page is loaded. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

Children

Bodai Sudoku is a general-audience puzzle game. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children; the play stats above are anonymous.

Changes

If the app ever adds features that use data, such as ads, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
