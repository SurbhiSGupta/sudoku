## Full description (max 4000 characters)

A clean, calm Sudoku made for your phone.

Every puzzle has exactly one solution, so you can always solve it with logic, never guesswork. Pick Easy, Medium or Hard and play at your own pace.

FEATURES
• Notes (pencil marks): jot down possible numbers, and they clear themselves as you fill the board
• Hints: stuck? Get the right number for the cell you've selected
• Mistake checking: see wrong numbers right away, or turn it off for a real challenge
• Undo and redo for every move
• Number First mode: pick a number, then tap every cell where it goes
• Tap any number to highlight everywhere it appears
• Number pad shows how many of each number are left
• Timer and progress tracking
• Your game saves automatically. Close the app and pick up right where you left off
• Gentle vibration when you place numbers and when you win
• Works fully offline

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no sign-up, no tracking. Your games stay on your phone.

Free to play.
